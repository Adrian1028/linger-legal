# Privacy Policy for Linger

**Effective date:** 19 April 2026
**Last updated:** 19 April 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Linger ("we", "us", "the app")
handles your information when you use our mobile application.

Linger is a habit tracker and journaling app. We designed it to
collect as little personal information as possible. This document
explains exactly what we collect, why, and how you can delete it.

## 1. Who we are

Linger is operated by Hok Lam Choy, trading as squarelol, based in
the United Kingdom.

Contact for privacy questions: fcsfcs007@gmail.com

## 2. What we collect and why

### 2.1 Account information (Supabase)

When you open Linger for the first time, we create an anonymous
account for you using Supabase Authentication. This account:

- Is identified by a random UUID generated by Supabase
- Is **not linked to your email, phone number, name, or any
  personal identifier**
- Exists so your habits and journal entries can sync across
  devices and survive app reinstalls

If you never create a non-anonymous account (we do not currently
offer sign-up with email), we never learn who you are.

### 2.2 App content (Supabase)

The following content you create in the app is stored in our
Supabase database on servers located in the European Union
(eu-west-1, Ireland):

- Habit names and completion records
- Journal entries (the text you write)
- App settings (reminder times, preferences)
- Subscription status (whether you are on Free or Premium)

This content is tied to your anonymous UUID. It is not read by
us, not shared with advertisers, and not used for training any
machine learning model.

### 2.3 Advertising (Google AdMob — Free users only)

If you are on the Free tier, Linger shows banner ads served by
Google AdMob. To serve these ads, AdMob collects:

- Your device's advertising identifier (AAID on Android)
- Approximate location (derived from IP address)
- Basic device information (model, OS version, language)
- Interaction data with the ad (impressions, clicks)

We do not receive any personal data from AdMob. Google uses this
data according to its own privacy practices. You can learn more
at Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads
in your device's Settings → Google → Ads.

If you upgrade to Linger Premium, ads are disabled entirely and
AdMob no longer loads.

### 2.4 Subscriptions (RevenueCat + Google Play Billing)

If you subscribe to Linger Premium, your purchase is processed
by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment
details.

To manage subscription state across devices, we use RevenueCat.
RevenueCat receives from us:

- Your anonymous Linger user ID
- A hashed Play Store purchase token

RevenueCat receives from Google Play:

- Subscription status (active, in trial, cancelled, etc.)
- Product purchased (monthly or yearly)
- Renewal and expiration dates

RevenueCat's privacy policy:
https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

### 2.5 What we do NOT collect

For clarity, Linger does not collect:

- Your name, email, or phone number (unless you contact us
  directly)
- Your contacts, photos, or files
- Your precise location (GPS)
- Your browsing history outside the app
- Biometric data
- Audio recordings
- Health or fitness data from HealthKit / Google Fit

## 3. How long we keep your data

We keep your habits, journal entries, and settings until you
delete your account. If you stop using the app without deleting
your account, your data remains in our database.

## 4. Deleting your data

You can delete all of your data from inside the app:

1. Open **Settings**
2. Tap **Delete account**
3. Confirm

This action:

- Permanently deletes your Supabase account and all linked data
  (habits, journal entries, settings)
- Is immediate and cannot be reversed
- Does not require us to contact you

This is implemented using a Supabase Edge Function called
`delete-user` that removes both your auth record and your
content in a single atomic operation.

If you cannot access the app (for example, you lost your
device), you can email us at fcsfcs007@gmail.com with enough
information to identify your account. Because accounts are
anonymous, in practice this means we may not be able to locate
your data without your device.

## 5. Sharing your data

We do not sell your data. We share data only with the service
providers listed above (Supabase, Google AdMob, RevenueCat,
Google Play) and only to the minimum extent necessary to
operate the app.

We may disclose data if compelled by law, court order, or in
response to a valid legal request from a UK or relevant foreign
authority.

## 6. Your rights (UK GDPR / EU GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the UK or EU, you have the right to:

- **Access** the data we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate data
- **Delete** your data (see Section 4)
- **Object** to processing
- **Data portability** — request an export of your data
- **Lodge a complaint** with the UK Information Commissioner's
  Office (ICO) at https://ico.org.uk, or your local EU data
  protection authority

If you are a California resident, you have similar rights under
the CCPA, including the right to know what personal information
is collected and the right to request deletion.

To exercise any of these rights, email fcsfcs007@gmail.com.

We do not knowingly sell personal information, and we do not
process personal information for cross-context behavioural
advertising beyond the standard AdMob banner behaviour described
in Section 2.3. If you wish to opt out of personalised ads, use
your device settings as described in that section.

## 7. Children

Linger is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the
EU, where applicable). We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. If you believe a child has used the
app, contact us and we will delete the associated data.

## 8. International transfers

Your data may be processed in the United Kingdom, the European
Union, and the United States (where Supabase, AdMob, and
RevenueCat operate servers). These transfers are protected by
standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, the UK
International Data Transfer Agreement.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes
will be communicated in the app before they take effect. The
"Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change.
